General

What is Rppl?
Rppl is an async-first, AI-powered collaboration platform that replaces tools like Zoom, Slack, Trello, Loom, and Otter. It helps fast-moving teams cut meetings, stay aligned, and make decisions faster — without the noise.
How is Rppl different from other collaboration tools?
Rppl flips the meeting model. Collaboration starts asynchronously. Live meetings happen only if needed. And everything is captured in a knowledge base, so nothing gets lost and your team can move faster, with fewer interruptions.
Who is Rppl for?
Rppl is built for high-performing teams overwhelmed by too many meetings and disconnected tools — especially in startups, product teams, agencies, and distributed organisations.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Rppl runs in your browser — no downloads required. We plan mobile and desktop apps in the future.
Can Rppl integrate with tools I already use?
We will be rolling out more integrations as time goes on.
What types of discussions work best on Rppl?
Rppl shines for focused decision-making, project updates, product planning, sprint reviews, roadmap alignment, hiring discussions — anything that clogs up your calendar but doesn't need a live meeting.
Can I still have live meetings with Rppl?
Yes, but you'll likely need fewer. Rppl helps you decide when a live meeting is actually necessary. It also preps you with the full context so you can skip the recap and jump straight to the decision.

AI & Knowledge Management

How does the AI work?
Our AI organizes and summarizes discussions, surfaces key insights, and helps you recall decisions, all within a private, secure workspace. It builds a knowledge lake from everything your team does.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. Rppl uses enterprise-grade encryption. Your knowledge lake is private to your organization and is not used to train external models. We are compliant with the industry standard SOC 2.
Can Rppl summarize long threads or discussions?
Yes. You can get an AI-generated summary of any discussion, decision, or topic thread — instantly. Great for catching up or getting new teammates up to speed.

Pricing & Access

How much does Rppl cost?
During our Beta period, Rppl is free for one month. We'll introduce transparent pricing after launch, with discounts for early adopters.
Can I invite my whole team?
Yes. During the Beta, there's no limit to team size.
Do you offer an enterprise version?
We're working with a few enterprise partners on custom deployments. If you're interested, reach out.

Getting Started

How do I sign up?
Join the waitlist and we'll be in touch.
Do you offer onboarding support?
Yes. We offer in platform support including a step-by-step guide, and contact us forms.
What should I try first in Rppl?
Start by using Rppl exactly how you run meetings today. Have a call, record discussions, capture notes, share decisions. Once you're comfortable, flip the workflow: try running your next meeting async-first. You'll be surprised how quickly your team aligns without ever jumping on a call.
