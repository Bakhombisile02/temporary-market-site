How Radley Works

R&D claims are stuck in the past. Radley is here to change that.

You know the feeling — the project wraps up, now comes the scramble: gathering docs, pulling reports, and tracking dev work just to file an R&D claim.

Every year, billions in innovation funding go unclaimed. Founders know it's undiluted capital, but the process is manual and confusing. It's built for accountants, not builders.

  • Teams spend weeks gathering screenshots, payroll data, and reports.
  • Claims happen once a year, long after the work is done.
  • Accountants cannot see code or context, which means guesswork and missed value.

Time wasted, opportunity lost, and avoidable risk.

How Radley works

Radley starts where engineering happens — your repositories and tools — so you stop chasing paperwork and start getting continuous, audit-ready insight.
