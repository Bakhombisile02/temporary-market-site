## The Flipped Meeting Revolution

Just as the flipped classroom transformed education by moving information delivery outside class time to focus on application and interaction, the Flipped Meeting Model transforms business collaboration.

**Traditional meetings** = information sharing + decision making + follow-up planning  
**Flipped Meetings** = informed participants + focused decision making + automated follow-through
